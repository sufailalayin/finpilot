## Full description
FinPilot by Hastron Ventures is a personal finance workspace designed to help you understand and manage your money in one place.

Track accounts and transactions, build budgets and savings goals, monitor bills and recurring commitments, understand your net worth, record assets and liabilities, and see a clear financial health score.

FinPilot Pro adds advanced financial intelligence including AI Copilot, smart budget forecasting, advanced reports, goal planning, investment and debt analytics, and proactive financial alerts.

### Core features
- Accounts, balances, income and expense tracking
- Transaction search, editing and transfers
- Categories and monthly budgets
- Savings goals and progress tracking
- Bills and recurring commitments
- Net worth overview
- Secure PIN and biometric app lock
- Data export and privacy controls

### FinPilot Pro
- AI Financial Copilot
- Financial Health Score 2.0
- Advanced reports and exports
- Smart budget forecasts and goal planning
- Assets, investments, loans and EMI analytics
- Smart financial alerts and cash-flow insights

FinPilot provides informational financial tools and does not guarantee investment, savings, lending, or other financial outcomes.
