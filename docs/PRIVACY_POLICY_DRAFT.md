# FinPilot Privacy Policy — Release Draft

**Product:** FinPilot  
**Publisher:** Hastron Ventures  
**Last updated:** 9 September 2026

FinPilot is a personal finance application designed to help users record and understand their financial information. This document is a release draft and should receive legal review before public publication.

## Information FinPilot may process

FinPilot may process account profile information, manually entered financial accounts, balances, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, recurring items, bills, subscriptions, assets, investments, liabilities, EMI information, AI questions, subscription status, security events, and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

FinPilot does not require bank-statement import or receipt scanning in the current product scope.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide the requested finance features, calculate dashboards and reports, generate financial health indicators, provide AI-assisted insights where enabled, manage subscriptions, secure accounts, troubleshoot the service, and comply with lawful obligations.

## AI features

FinPilot Pro may send relevant user financial context and the user's question to the configured AI service so that the requested response can be generated. FinPilot should only send information required for the feature being used. AI output is informational and is not a guarantee of financial outcomes.

## Security

FinPilot uses authenticated API access, encrypted token storage on supported devices, optional PIN or biometric app lock, session revocation, audit logging, and production configuration checks. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## Data export and deletion

Users can export a copy of their FinPilot data from Security & Privacy. Users can also request permanent account deletion from within the app after password confirmation. Deletion removes the user account and data linked by database cascade rules, subject to any information that must legally be retained.

## Payments

Paid FinPilot subscriptions on Android are processed through Google Play. Google may process payment and purchase information under its own privacy terms. FinPilot receives subscription verification information needed to determine entitlement status.

## Retention

FinPilot retains user data while an account remains active and as needed to provide the service, secure the platform, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Deleted-account handling must follow the final production retention policy approved by Hastron Ventures.

## Children

FinPilot is intended for users who are legally able to use personal financial services under the rules applicable to their location. The production release should define and enforce the final age requirement.

## Contact

Privacy contact: **info@hastron.in**

Before publication, Hastron Ventures should verify the contact details, retention periods, legal basis, age requirement, third-party processors, hosting regions, and all disclosures required by applicable law and Google Play.
